Coffee and appliances

Sage espresso machine

Check the water tank and bean hopper. Switch on, grind beans into the portafilter, tamp evenly, lock the portafilter into the group head and select espresso on the touchscreen. For milk drinks use the milk jug and steam wand. If the machine requests cleaning, descaling or filter replacement, postpone it if permitted and let us know; we will take care of it. Do not force a blocked machine.

Nespresso

Fill the water tank, switch on, insert a capsule, close the lever and select your preferred coffee size. Dispose of used capsules in the appropriate container.

Oven, induction hob, dishwasher and washing machine

Please follow the appliance controls and use suitable detergent. Switch everything off after use. Contact us if anything is unclear.

Sonos Era 100 – Bluetooth

Enable Bluetooth on your phone. Hold the Bluetooth button on the back of the speaker for about two seconds until the light flashes blue. Select “Sonos Era 100” on your phone. A solid blue light indicates connection. Alternatively, connect to the apartment Wi-Fi and use the Sonos app to join an existing system. Do not reset the speaker.

07

Safety and electricity

Netatmo smoke detector

If the alarm sounds, first check for fire. If there are flames, heavy smoke or any uncertainty, leave immediately and call 112. Only if you are certain it is harmless cooking smoke: turn off the source, open windows and briefly press the front of the detector to silence it temporarily. Contact us. Never remove or disable the detector.

Fire extinguisher and emergencies

An extinguisher is provided. Only use it if safe and you know how. For urgent danger or medical emergencies call 112. For ordinary issues, message us.

Electrical panel

If electricity fails, first check the apartment electrical panel. If power is still off, contact us before attempting any reset at the meter in the cellar. Do not touch wiring.

08

Before you leave

Check-out checklist

Please dispose of rubbish, empty the fridge of your food and leave the apartment reasonably tidy. Close windows, switch off lights and appliances, and sign out of streaming apps. Professional cleaning is provided; you do not need to clean the apartment.

Return the keys

Leave the barrier remote and second set of keys next to the thermostat. Take the main keys with you, close the apartment door, then return the main keys to the keybox inside the RIGHT letterbox. Check that the keybox is securely locked.
