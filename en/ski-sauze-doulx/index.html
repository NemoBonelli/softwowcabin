Skiing the Vialattea

Sauze d’Oulx is one of the gateways to the Vialattea, the international ski area connecting Sauze, Sestriere, Sansicario, Cesana, Claviere, Pragelato and Montgenèvre in France.

Set up for a full ski week

The ski & bike room provides space for skis, helmets and equipment and includes a boot dryer. The private parking space is covered and has an EV wallbox. Inside there is a fully equipped kitchen, Wi‑Fi, a double bedroom and a double sofa bed in the living room.

Ideal for two, sleeping up to four

For a couple, the 40 sqm layout provides a separate bedroom and a living space to enjoy throughout the day. When needed, the double sofa bed allows the apartment to sleep up to four guests.

Grand Villard and Sauze centre

Soft Wow Cabin is in Grand Villard. The centre of Sauze d’Oulx is around a 15-minute walk away on a substantially level route, while the apartment sits in a quieter, greener setting.

Getting here

Sauze can be reached by road through the Susa Valley and from France. Oulx is the nearest railway station, around 10 km away, with onward local buses and taxis.
